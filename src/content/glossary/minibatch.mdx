**What it is.** A list $B$ of $b$ indices drawn at random from $\{1, \dots, n\}$, used to estimate the gradient of the empirical risk by the average $g_B = \frac1b \sum_{i \in B} \nabla \ell_i(\theta)$. Batch size $b = 1$ is plain SGD; $b = n$ (every example once) is full-batch gradient descent.

**How ML uses it.** When $B$ is drawn uniformly, $g_B$ is an unbiased estimate of $\nabla \hat R(\theta)$, and when its $b$ indices are drawn independently its covariance is the single-example covariance divided by $b$ (for $b$ distinct indices, a subset, multiply by $(n-b)/(n-1)$). Larger batches give less noisy steps at a higher cost per step; on a GPU, a batch of 32 to 512 examples often costs little more than one. An epoch is one pass through the data in minibatches.
